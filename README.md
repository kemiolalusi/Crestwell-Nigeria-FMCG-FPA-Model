# Crestwell FMCG FP&A Analytics

## Project Overview

An FP&A analytics and decision-support demo built to model a realistic multinational FMCG business.

The project combines financial and commercial analysis with SQL Server and Power BI data modelling and visualization to transform transactional and planning data into management insights across performance, profitability, variance analysis, price-volume-mix, forecasting and scenario planning.

The model is designed to answer three core questions:

- **What is happening?**
- **Why is it happening?**
- **Where might management attention be required?**

---

## Business Context

Crestwell is a fictional multinational FMCG business created for this portfolio project.

The model focuses was designed around a realistic FMCG commercial environment, including multiple customer channels, outlet tiers, geographic markets, product categories, brands and products.

The purpose was not to recreate the financials of a specific real company, but to create a realistic business environment in which FP&A and business analytics questions could be explored using transactional and planning data.

## Business Questions

The analysis was designed around questions an FP&A team could use to support management decisions.

### Performance

- How is revenue and profitability developing over time?
- How does actual performance compare with budget?
- How are gross profit and margins changing?

### Revenue Drivers

- Is revenue growth being driven by volume, price or mix?
- Are changes in average selling price translating into profitable growth?
- What is contributing to changes in revenue?

### Commercial Performance

- Which channels contribute most to revenue?
- Which geographic markets contribute most to revenue?
- How does product and brand mix vary across the business?

### Cost Management

- How is OPEX developing relative to revenue?
- Which cost categories are driving expenditure?
- How does actual OPEX compare with budget?

### Scenario Analysis

- How would different revenue growth assumptions affect projected revenue?
- How would changes in growth affect gross profit?
- What could operating profit look like under different growth scenarios?
- How sensitive are projected financial outcomes to changes in the revenue growth assumption?

## Key FP&A Analysis

### Revenue & Profitability

The Power BI model analyzes:

- Revenue trends
- Gross profit
- Gross profit margin
- Revenue by category
- Revenue by brand
- Revenue by channel
- Revenue by geography

### Price-Volume-Mix Analysis

The PVM analysis decomposes revenue movement into:

- Volume impact
- Price impact
- Mix / interaction

This helps distinguish growth generated by selling more units from growth associated with changes in average selling price.

### Budget & Variance Analysis

The model compares actual performance against budget and uses variance analysis to investigate where performance differs from plan.

Because the supplied budget data is available at monthly company level, budget variance analysis is restricted to dimensions supported by the available budget grain rather than artificially allocating budget across channels, locations or other dimensions.

### OPEX & Cost Management

The model evaluates:

- OPEX trends
- OPEX by category
- Actual vs budget OPEX
- OPEX as a percentage of revenue

### Scenario Analysis

The Scenario Analysis page uses a Power BI What-If parameter to test how changes in revenue growth assumptions could affect projected financial outcomes.

The analysis allows management to explore:

- Revenue growth scenarios
- Scenario Revenue
- Scenario Gross Profit
- Scenario Operating Profit
- The potential effect of different growth assumptions on financial performance

---

## Key Findings

The analysis highlights several management-relevant patterns within the simulated dataset:

- Revenue increases substantially across the 2021–2025 period while gross profit margin also improved.
- Revenue growth is influenced by both volume and pricing, which are separated through the PVM analysis.
- Lagos represents the largest commercial market in the dataset, followed by other major markets including FCT, Kano and Rivers.
- The model allows management to investigate revenue concentration across channels, products, brands and geographic markets.
- OPEX can be evaluated against revenue and budget to identify changes in cost intensity.
- Scenario analysis demonstrates how changes in revenue growth assumptions can affect projected financial outcomes.

These findings are based on the simulated Crestwell dataset and should not be interpreted as actual financial results of any real company.

## Data & Methodology

The project uses simulated data created for analytical and portfolio purposes.

The business structure, commercial dimensions and planning framework were designed to reflect a realistic Nigerian FMCG environment.

The model includes sales, customers, products, locations, sales agents, expenses, budgets, forecasts and long-range planning assumptions.

Data validation was performed before the analytical layer was built, including checks for duplicate records, missing dimensional references and calculation integrity.


## Important Note

Crestwell is a fictional company created for this portfolio project.

All financial and commercial data is simulated and does not represent confidential or proprietary information from  anyreal company.

The project was designed to demonstrate financial analysis, FP&A thinking, SQL, Power BI, data modelling and decision-support capabilities.
